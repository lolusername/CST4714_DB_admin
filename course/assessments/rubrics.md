# Common Rubrics

## Individual Lab Rubric: 10 Points

| Criterion | Points | What to look for |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical task | 4 | required query, document, trace, or design is correct for the supplied case |
| Verification | 3 | student shows how they know the result is correct |
| Explanation | 2 | student connects the result to the operational question |
| Safe and reproducible practice | 1 | another reader can follow the reasoning or code; secrets and private data are absent |

When a platform outage blocks execution, the lab's documented fallback can earn the
same points.

A paper trace or writing lab does not require executable code. Grade the stated
learning task, not an unassigned tool or file format. Where an explanation is
embedded in a lab, use the writing criteria below to give feedback within the
lab's explanation points; do not grade the same response as an extra assignment.

## Critical Writing Rubric: 10 Points

| Criterion | Points | What to look for |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct claim | 2 | response answers the prompt rather than only summarizing |
| Specific support | 3 | answer is tied to a result, test, example, or cited source |
| Technical accuracy | 2 | database concepts are used correctly |
| Tradeoff or limitation | 2 | response identifies a real cost, risk, or uncertainty |
| Clear communication | 1 | the intended reader can understand the reasoning; specialized terms are used only where helpful |

Do not award points for jargon or require students to imply professional
experience they do not have. A technically accurate explanation in plain language
can earn full credit. Grammar feedback should not replace feedback on database
reasoning.

## Notebook Rubric: 10 Points

| Criterion | Points | What to look for |
|---|---:|---|
| Cells run in order | 3 | outputs match the stated purpose or fallback path |
| Student modification | 3 | learner changes data, query, or parameter rather than only pressing Run |
| Interpretation | 3 | learner explains what one output shows and what the exercise does not cover |
| Credential safety | 1 | secrets are entered at runtime and never saved in the notebook |
