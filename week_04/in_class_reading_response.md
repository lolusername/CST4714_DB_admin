## Activity Type
In-class individual response (participation credit)

## Time
15-20 minutes

## Goal
Show that you can explain core Week 4 ideas in your own words using documentation.

## Submission Format
Create `week4_reading_response.md` and answer all prompts.

For each prompt:
- 90-140 words
- include one source citation line (section or page/heading)

Total target: 300-450 words.

---

## Prompts

### Prompt 1
Explain one practical reason to use a **view** instead of repeating the same join in many queries.

### Prompt 2
Explain the difference between **identity-generated IDs** and the idea of perfectly gap-free numbering.

### Prompt 3
Choose one constraint type (`CHECK`, `UNIQUE`, or `FOREIGN KEY`) and explain one bad write it prevents.

---

## Scoring (Participation / 10)
- 4 pts: all prompts answered
- 3 pts: citations included
- 3 pts: answers are clear and practical (not copied text)

No credit if most content is copied without interpretation.
