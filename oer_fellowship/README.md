# OER Fellowship Work

This directory explains, evaluates, and prepares the OER for review. It is **not**
the student course itself.

## Read Only This First

1. [How to present the project](HOW_TO_PRESENT_THIS_PROJECT.md)
2. [Current scope and quality evidence](evidence/FELLOWSHIP_SCOPE_EVIDENCE.md)

Those two files distinguish what was authored, what was revised, and what is
still under review. The [screenshot showcase](showcase/SHOWCASE.md) and older QA
records document earlier drafts; their figures and test results do not certify
the September 7 revisions. Refresh the chosen examples before a formal presentation.

The current book review copy is the
[second-edition Word/PDF/HTML/EPUB workspace](../course/textbook/publishing/README.md). The
first-edition source and exports remain preserved for comparison; the second
edition is a revision of the same 15-chapter OER, not a second course counted to
inflate the fellowship scope.

## Supporting Records

| Folder | What is inside | When to open it |
|---|---|---|
| [`planning/`](planning/) | fellowship scope, production/evaluation plan, and deliverable register | when discussing why the project exists, what was planned, or how work is counted |
| [`evidence/`](evidence/) | currentness research, pedagogy, accessibility QA, publication QA, cloud checks, and scope evidence | when a reviewer asks how a quality or educational claim is supported |
| [`release/`](release/) | pilot packet, external-review gates, and deposit metadata | after approval, when preparing review, pilot, or publication |
| [`reference/`](reference/) | the long presentation script, full question bank, and detailed QA narrative | when the short guide is not enough |
| [`showcase/`](showcase/) | captioned screenshots of representative course artifacts and current cloud interfaces | when presenting the work visually |

## Boundary

- Fellowship planning and evidence are deliberately separate from student OER.
- Created course OER is cataloged in the [Catalog of Created OER](../course/OER_CATALOG.md).
- Free vendor material is cataloged in the [free external resource catalog](../course/external_resources/INDEX.md).
- Fellowship plans and QA records explain the work but are not counted as student
  instructional OER.
- The package remains an unpublished draft until the required review,
  pilot, revision, approval, and publication steps are complete.
