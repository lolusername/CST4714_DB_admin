## The Contribution

I am developing *Operating Cloud Databases*, an open textbook and coordinated
teaching package built from my earlier CST4714 course. This packet demonstrates
the completed first-eight-weeks revision: relational and SQL review, schema
integrity, safe changes, transactions, permissions, indexing, and recovery.
The broader fifteen-chapter draft exists separately; later materials are not
included or represented as reviewed in this packet.

The contribution is the authored explanation, worked examples and
counterexamples, synthetic fixtures, executable student adaptations, integrated
writing prompts, and instructor guidance. PostgreSQL, Supabase, MongoDB,
vendor documentation, and vendor-owned lessons are external resources, not
original fellowship output. Existing teaching material is a revision baseline;
unchanged copies and different export formats are not counted as new works.

## A Short Demonstration for Reviewers

Open the [course map](README.md), then show one sequence rather than every file.

1. [Chapter 3](textbook/module_03_schema.md) explains constraints and their limits.
   The [Week 3 deck](weeks/week_03/week_03_schemas_constraints_integrity.pptx)
   works through accepted and rejected writes. The individual lab asks students
   to adapt the rule and explain a consequence for a person using the application.
2. [Week 7](weeks/week_07/README.md) teaches an index as an experiment: preserve
   result IDs, compare plan work, measure storage, and recommend a decision.
   It does not reward an unsupported claim that one noisy timing proves success.
3. [Week 8](weeks/week_08/README.md) connects the reading to a real restore and
   shows why equal row counts can conceal a wrong value. Students adapt one
   query and explain their result in the same notebook, without a second report.

These examples show rigorous beginner instruction: terminology and mechanisms
precede independent work; students modify a small worked case rather than
designing an unexplained complex application. Writing addresses actual query
results and operating decisions rather than generic corporate reflections.
