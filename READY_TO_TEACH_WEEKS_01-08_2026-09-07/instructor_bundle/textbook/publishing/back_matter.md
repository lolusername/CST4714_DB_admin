## Companion Materials {.unnumbered #companion-materials}

The accompanying course folder contains eight weekly guides, fourteen individual
labs, three notebooks, editable PowerPoints, PDF slide handouts, transcripts,
the Metro Support fixture, an optional CISA sample, and the midterm assignment.
Open the course README to find the relevant week. In an EPUB reader, download
and open the corresponding notebook from that companion folder rather than
expecting an EPUB to execute code. No public download address is assumed before
the instructor approves publication.
