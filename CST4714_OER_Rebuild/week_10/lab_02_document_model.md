# Lab 2: Translate One Workload Into a Document Model

## Purpose

Apply access-pattern reasoning to the Metro Support relationships after completing
a distinct MongoDB University activity.

This is individual work completed in class. Submit one Brightspace text response.

## 1. Complete the Free External Activity

Complete [Relational (SQL) to Document Model](https://learn.mongodb.com/courses/relational-to-document-model).

This is not the **Modeling Data Relationships** activity used by the instructor
for live demonstration.

Keep one completion screen that shows the activity title and your completion
status or score. Do not expose your email address, account identifier, cluster URI,
or any credential.

## 2. Apply the Reasoning to Metro Support

Choose one workload:

- ticket page with latest events;
- agent queue with ticket summaries;
- cross-ticket event analytics; or
- resident history with requester details.

Write one representative MongoDB document in strict Extended JSON-compatible
form. Use embedding, referencing, or a simple hybrid. Then explain:

- the chosen access pattern;
- what is embedded and why;
- what is referenced and why;
- expected array/document growth;
- duplicated data and its authoritative owner; and
- one query the model makes harder.

## Submit One Thing

In one Brightspace text submission, paste the redacted completion image, JSON
model, and 150-250 word claim-evidence-tradeoff explanation. Do not submit a second
file or repository.
