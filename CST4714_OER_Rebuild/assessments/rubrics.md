# Common Rubrics

## Individual Lab Rubric: 10 Points

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Correct execution | 4 | artifact runs or parses; required result is present |
| Verification | 3 | student shows how they know the result is correct |
| Explanation | 2 | student connects evidence to the operational question |
| Safe and reproducible practice | 1 | steps are ordered; secrets and private data are absent |

When a platform outage blocks execution, the lab's fallback evidence can earn the
same points.

## Critical Writing Rubric: 10 Points

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct claim | 2 | response answers the prompt rather than only summarizing |
| Specific evidence | 3 | claim is tied to a result, test, example, or cited source |
| Technical accuracy | 2 | database concepts are used correctly |
| Tradeoff or limitation | 2 | response identifies a real cost, risk, or uncertainty |
| Professional clarity | 1 | writing is concise, readable, and appropriate for the genre |

## Notebook Evidence Rubric: 10 Points

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Cells run in order | 3 | outputs match the stated purpose or fallback path |
| Student modification | 3 | learner changes data, query, or parameter rather than only pressing Run |
| Interpretation | 3 | learner explains what one output proves and what it does not prove |
| Credential safety | 1 | secrets are entered at runtime and never saved in the notebook |
