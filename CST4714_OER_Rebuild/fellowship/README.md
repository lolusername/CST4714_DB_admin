# OER Fellowship Planning and Reporting

This directory contains project-management, evaluation, production, and reporting
documents for the OER fellowship. It is deliberately separate from the student
course materials in the parent directory.

## Planning Documents

- [Fellowship project plan](OER_FELLOWSHIP_PROJECT_PLAN.md): rationale, scope,
  pedagogy, accessibility, publication, schedule, and dissemination.
- [Production and evaluation plan](PRODUCTION_AND_EVALUATION_PLAN.md): quality
  gates, classroom evidence cycle, decision rules, and data stewardship.
- [Deliverable register](OER_DELIVERABLE_REGISTER.md): planned IDs, production
  categories, licenses, and release-accounting rules.
- [Fellowship scope and quality evidence](FELLOWSHIP_SCOPE_EVIDENCE.md):
  criterion-by-criterion evidence, observable scope, honest gaps, and a concise
  presentation structure.
- [Currentness, pedagogy, and QA audit](CURRENTNESS_PEDAGOGY_QA_AUDIT.md):
  current primary-source baseline, learning-design evidence, optional-extension
  inventory, locally controllable checks, and external release gates.
- [Standalone HTML accessibility QA](STANDALONE_HTML_ACCESSIBILITY_QA.md):
  dated semantic, reflow, and representative Safari keyboard evidence with
  explicit limits and a platform-retest protocol.
- [Publication format QA](PUBLICATION_FORMAT_QA.md): independent EPUBCheck
  conformance, corrected presentation metadata, PDF tag-tree findings, and
  explicit accessibility claim boundaries.
- [Cloud and vendor smoke-test record](CLOUD_AND_VENDOR_SMOKE_TEST_RECORD.md):
  completed account-free checks, exact live protocols, and honestly unrun
  account-gated evidence.
- [Pilot evidence packet](PILOT_EVIDENCE_PACKET.md): evaluation questions,
  instruments, decision rules, aggregate evidence tables, and a revision record
  without invented classroom results.
- [External review and release gates](EXTERNAL_REVIEW_AND_RELEASE_GATES.md):
  attribution, accessibility, Pressbooks, approval, tagging, deposit, and
  publication boundaries.
- [Publication and deposit metadata](PUBLICATION_DEPOSIT_METADATA.md): prepared
  Pressbooks, OpenLab, Academic Works, citation, rights, and version fields.

## Evidence of Created OER

- [Catalog of created OER](../OER_CATALOG.md): exact artifacts, links, original
  contributions, licenses, and current completion status.
- [Free external resource catalog](../FREE_EXTERNAL_RESOURCES.md): no-cost vendor
  platforms, documentation, and activities that are useful to the course but are
  not claimed as fellowship-created OER.
- [Attributions](../ATTRIBUTIONS.md): open sources, external references, learning
  design evidence, career sources, and dataset provenance.

The distinction supports a clear fellowship report: the plan explains the work;
the OER catalog shows what was created; and the external-resource catalog shows
what was only curated or linked.
