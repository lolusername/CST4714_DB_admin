## Executive Finding

*Operating Cloud Databases* is credibly a highest-scope fellowship proposal. It
does not merely replace a textbook link or add a few assignments. It creates a
primary 15-module open text and a coordinated course implementation package
containing individual labs, executable notebooks, reusable datasets,
assessments, projects, slide decks, complete spoken scripts, alternate formats,
instructor implementation guidance, and automated quality checks.

The official City Tech OER Fellowship call states that project stipends may
range from $1,300 to $6,000 depending on scope. It does not publish a formula or
promise that a particular inventory receives the maximum. The project should be
presented as a full-course transformation with several distinct forms of
intellectual and technical work. The sponsor, not this self-audit, determines
the award.

Do not claim a particular number of development hours unless supported by real
contemporaneous records. The stronger argument is the observable scope,
original educational design, technical execution, accessibility work, and
reusability documented below.

## Alignment With Official Fellowship Priorities

The 2026-27 call encourages active learning, student-centered projects, and work
in underrepresented or multidisciplinary areas. It requires open licensing,
accessibility, and public CUNY-supported publication. It asks applicants to
identify a primary course material and up to four kinds of supporting material.

This project supplies:

1. **A primary course material:** a coherent open textbook and lab manual rather
   than a list of vendor tutorials.
2. **Study guides and topical modules:** 15 sequenced modules and weekly guides.
3. **Assignments and assessments:** 25 labs, writing tasks, retrieval checks,
   diagnostic/post instruments, rubrics, midterm, and final.
4. **Lecture notes and lesson plans:** complete public implementation guidance
   for two meetings per week.
5. **Slide decks and original visuals:** 214 student-facing slides with complete
   word-for-word scripts and alternate study formats.

The project also adds six executable notebooks, three data packages, cloud/local
equivalence, career communication, and reproducible validation. Those items make
the proposal broader than the four supporting-material checkboxes alone suggest.

## Why the Work Is Substantial

The visible files represent six different kinds of work that are often hidden
when a project is summarized only as "course materials":

### 1. Curriculum Architecture

The syllabus, outcome map, assessment map, 15-week progression, recurring case,
relational re-entry bridge, and evidence cycle had to agree. This is course
design, not file conversion.

### 2. Original Instructional Authorship

The modules, worked examples, misconceptions, labs, assignments, retrieval
questions, rubrics, transcripts, and lesson plans use consistent beginner
language while preserving technical accuracy.

### 3. Technical Development

The project includes executable SQL, MQL, JSON, Python, Jupyter notebooks,
credential-safe cloud paths, local fallbacks, deterministic datasets, and a
public validation tool. The notebook and SQL evidence is actually executed, not
merely displayed as sample code.

### 4. Media and Multi-Format Production

Fifteen PowerPoint decks, 214 slide layouts, native diagrams, speaker notes,
matching transcripts, PDF handouts, a publication cover, and web/EPUB/Word
exports form a substantial media-production stream.

### 5. Accessibility and Access Design

The work includes structured text alternatives, cloud/local equivalence,
descriptive links, predictable headings, large high-contrast slides, no paid
dependency, and guidance for cognitive-load and access barriers. The remaining
platform audit is documented rather than hidden.

### 6. Quality Assurance and Sustainability

The package validates inventory, canonical assignments, links, notebooks,
datasets, SQL, credentials, privacy exclusions, slide boundaries, PDF page
counts, and exact notes/transcript identity. Editable sources and documented
rebuild commands support future adoption rather than a one-time course shell.

## Two-Minute Presentation Structure

### Problem

Students entered database administration having forgotten core SQL, while the
available materials split relational review, cloud interfaces, MongoDB, and
administration into disconnected product tutorials.

### Design Response

I created one lab-first open course around a repeated professional cycle:
identify the system promise, inspect evidence, make one controlled change, and
verify the result. The first modules rebuild relational algebra and SQL before
the course asks students to administer PostgreSQL and MongoDB systems.

### Scale

The project includes a 15-module text, 25 individual labs, six executable
notebooks, three data packages, two major projects, a complete assessment set,
15 student slide decks with 214 slides and exact scripts, alternate study
formats, and public implementation guidance.

### Educational Contribution

The original contribution is not simply coverage of two products. It is the
coherent bridge from relational reasoning to operational evidence, document
modeling, recovery, reliability, scale, and career communication. Every graded
activity produces one manageable artifact that a student can explain and an
instructor can inspect.

### Sustainability

The editable package separates concepts from changing vendor interfaces,
provides no-cost fallback paths, and includes automated checks for links, code,
slides, notes, data, and private information. The remaining fellowship work is
publication review, accessible-platform validation, classroom implementation,
and evidence-based revision.

## Claims to Avoid

- Do not say the project took a specific number of full-time months without
  records supporting that statement.
- Do not call the PDFs tagged or fully accessible before a tag and reading-order
  audit.
- Do not claim external peer review, student validation, or learning gains before
  those activities occur.
- Do not count MongoDB University, vendor documentation, cloud accounts, or CISA
  source records as fellowship-created OER.
- Do not guarantee the maximum stipend; demonstrate scope and let the fellowship
  team apply its compensation process.
