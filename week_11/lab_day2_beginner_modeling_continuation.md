# Week 11 Lab Day 2
## MongoDB University In-Class Lab: Improving Performance of `$sort` Stages

## Purpose
Day 2 is now a short in-class MongoDB University lab instead of a longer custom lab.

The goal is to give students one guided look at a real MongoDB performance idea without turning the whole week into an advanced tuning unit.

## Big Idea
Even at a beginner level, students can start to see that:
- stage order matters
- broad work is more expensive than narrow work
- and reducing the data that must be sorted can improve performance

This is a preview topic, not a mastery topic.

## Individual Work Only
This lab is individual.
There is no group work.

## In Class Lab
Complete this official MongoDB University lab during class:

- [Improving Performance of `$sort` stages (Lab Only)](https://learn.mongodb.com/courses/improving-performance-of-sort-stages-lab-only)

MongoDB University describes this lab as helping students learn how to improve the performance of `$sort` by reducing the dataset to sort.

## What Students Should Focus On
As you complete the lab, pay attention to:
1. what the sort stage is doing
2. why a large sort can be expensive
3. how filtering or reducing the dataset earlier can help
4. how this connects to the idea that workload shape matters

## What Students Should Submit

1. one screenshot showing the MongoDB University lab completion or progress

## Success Standard
Day 2 is successful if students can explain, in plain language, why sorting less data is usually better than sorting more data.

## What Strong Day 2 Work Shows
- the student completed the official lab in class
- the student can explain that stage order affects work
- the student can connect this lab to the broader idea that MongoDB query shape matters
