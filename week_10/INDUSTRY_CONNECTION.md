# Industry / Career Connection: Forbes: a document model supported changing content

**Primary source:** [MongoDB customer account about Forbes, 2020 (vendor-published)](https://www.mongodb.com/company/blog/innovation/forbes-cloud-migration-helps-worlds-biggest-media-brand-continue-standard-digital-innovation). External content retains its own terms.

## What the organization reported

MongoDB’s customer account describes Forbes using a document-based content system and later moving transactional workloads to Atlas and Google Cloud. It attributes flexibility to the content model. This is a vendor-published account, not an independent performance benchmark.

## Connection to this lesson

Flexible documents still require deliberate field meanings, precise filters, and checks after writes. Our ticket examples provide those concrete checks; the case does not establish a particular schema for Forbes.

The connection above is our instructional interpretation of the source, not a claim that the company used our Metro Support design.

## A short application

Imagine an article document with status and a small tags array. State a filter for one intended article, explain the difference between an omitted projection field and missing stored data, and identify evidence that a repeated update made no further change. This is an invented transfer example, not Forbes’s published schema.
