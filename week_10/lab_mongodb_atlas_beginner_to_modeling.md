# Week 10 Lab
## MongoDB University Lab Track: Beginner to Data Modeling

## Purpose
This week should be centered on MongoDB University, not a custom instructor-built lab.

The goal is to move students through MongoDB's official beginner learning path far enough that they:
- get comfortable inside Atlas,
- understand the document model,
- practice relationship modeling,
- see how schema validation fits into a flexible-schema system,
- and connect indexing to real query patterns.

## Big Idea
Week 10 is not about covering every MongoDB feature.
It is about spending real time in the official MongoDB beginner labs and practices so students move from:
- beginner Atlas use,
- to document thinking,
- to actual data-modeling decisions.
